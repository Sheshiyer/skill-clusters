## Workflow distilled

1. Generate one strong combined vertical image in GPT Image 2: character design sheet + storyboard page.
2. Upload that board into Seedance 2 canvas.
3. Use the uploaded storyboard as a strict reference.
4. Generate a short, emotionally coherent anime scene with timeline, SFX, camera notes, and negative constraints.
5. Reuse the rewrite prompt to adapt the structure to new original ideas.
